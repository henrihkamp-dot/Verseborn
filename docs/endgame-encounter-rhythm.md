# Stages 51-60: Encounter Rhythm

Release 132 keeps player gear, talents, mana rules, enemy HP and the 20-round limit intact.
Preparations remain visible. Successful interruption opens a recovery opportunity; control does not make every enemy harmless.
Escorts add a light group attack on their second action and every third action thereafter, unless their immediate healing/protection duty takes priority. Killing or controlling escorts removes that pressure. Group attacks do not redirect through Taunt; ordinary single-target attacks still do.

## Progression

The existing first and second phase formations teach combinations before the boss phase. Healing and protection duties take precedence over incidental group pressure. Root/Oath/Inspection/Veil recovery actions now retain light group pressure rather than becoming entirely passive windows.

No new status icons or VFX sheets are required. New named actions reuse existing elements, statuses and animation paths.

## Verification And Limits

Focused tests cover all thirty formations, real group-hit resolution through Taunt, temporary blessing/guest buffs, physical versus magical interruption, existing preparation/recovery cycles, and replay pressure.
These are engine regression tests, not a claim that every party and gear level now has identical difficulty. Mythic Stage-54 reports remain the practical playtest reference; fully optimized Artifact builds are intentionally allowed to overpower encounters.
