# Torren shield and Fault Line correction

Fault Line advertised 1.25 STR + 0.625 STAM but inherited the 0.1 stamina contribution of ordinary shield attacks. Give this skill explicit staminaScaling 0.5; its coefficient then produces the advertised formula. Normal attacks, Foundation Break and other heroes retain their scaling. Both previews and combat use skillOffensiveStat.

Worldroot Impact on Stoneheart Worldroot Shield adds 30% Stun for 2 actions to basic attacks and STR-scaled damaging skills, subject to native resistance/duration rules. Keep its basic-attack barrier and Guard bonus. Stats, affixes, art and Destiny Ring II are unchanged.

At Stage 60, a one-time save migration equips all five Personal Artifact slots for recruited party members, reusing owned pieces and filling missing pieces. Back up the original save, persist a per-save marker, preserve every Destiny Ring II reference and custom gear choices on subsequent loads. Manual set grant also stamps the marker.

Rift pools remain 32 nonweapons in Rifts 1–9, all 8 weapons in Rift 10. First clears and rematches use the same authoritative catalogue and prioritize missing items.
