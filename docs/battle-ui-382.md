# Healing-only tab and stacked action icons

Move all pure protective/self-guard buffs and cleanse-only support to Buff & Debuff. Basic retains basic attacks and native Defend. Healing actions contains HP heals and MP restoration only; healing skills with secondary protective effects retain their primary healing placement. Ultimates stay in their dedicated tab. Preserve the existing internal category key for remembered selection compatibility.

Make action artwork 66–76px with centered skill names and cost/target text underneath. Keep the borderless treatment and compact pagination; adjust dock height just enough to fit labels without clipping.

Weapon follow-up: default weapon status procs and legacy weapon effects now work on basic attacks and STR-scaled damage skills, including physical ultimates. Positive/self weapon effects apply once per action, not per area target; status procs remain per eligible surviving target. Coatings and basic-only talents keep their own trigger rules. Intrinsic Basic Attack MP recovery is unchanged.

Torren Hearthstone adds 30% Siphoning plus a minimum 3 MP refund for physical damaging skills, capped at MP spent and available MP space. The minimum is a personal bonus, not a change to global Siphoning. No refunds for basics, Ultimates, misses or zero-damage actions. Existing neck healing bonus and five affixes preserved. Existing equipped copies and Rift drops resolve the same catalogue.
