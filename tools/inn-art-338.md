# Inn Between Worlds artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Retained existing guest designs. Compressed WebP assets live in `public/game/assets/lost-dimensions/` and the deployment copy in `dist/client/game/assets/lost-dimensions/`.

- `materials-338.webp`: four texture panels: timber-and-stone pub walls with tankard shelves and candles; overhead worn oak floor; hearth wall; wine cellar wall. Rich fantasy comic ink illustration, warm amber, burgundy, brass and plum shadows; no characters or interface.
- `doors-338.webp`: two frontal doorway panels: carved oak door with brass bell and purple stained glass; ornate brass clockwork portal showing a violet nebula, floating islands and a distant inn. Same candlelit fantasy comic finish, no characters or readable text.
- `directions-338.webp`: three-column directional sheet, front, right side and back. Top row: the same gaunt human debt collector in a dark plum embroidered coat and tall hat, holding a ledger. Bottom row: the same carved wooden lost-property trunk on stout wooden feet, iron straps and brass lock. Full figures, grounded feet, consistent proportions, amber rim lighting, transparent cutouts. Follow-up edit removed background and haze while preserving identities and arrangement. Cropped into normalized cells for the game.
- `guests-337.webp`: existing landlord, breakfast guest and creditor artwork retained and recompressed.

Original generated images remain in the Codex generated-images directory. Deployment art totals approximately 252 KB. Directional figures use viewpoint-dependent images rather than pretending to be 3D models. Tables, counters and benches use projected solid faces with illustrated material textures. Flames, portal light, magical motes, clock pendulum and subtle intact-silhouette breathing are rendered separately.
